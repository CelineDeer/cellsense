CellSense – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 6 October 2026

CellSense (“the app”) is published by Kairos. This policy explains what the app does with data. In short: everything stays on your phone unless you switch on Remote monitoring.

Data that stays on your phone

  • Readings from your battery management system (BMS) received over Bluetooth: voltages, current, temperatures, state of charge, alarms and device information such as model and serial number.
  • The names you give your batteries and their Bluetooth addresses.
  • Reading history (kept for 90 days) and your settings.

This data is stored only in the app’s private storage on your device. It is not sent to us or to anyone else. Uninstalling the app deletes it. CSV exports are created only when you ask for one and are saved where you choose.

Permissions

  • Nearby devices (Bluetooth) – to find your BMS and read data from it. The app declares that it does not derive location from Bluetooth scans.
  • Location (Android 11 and older only) – these Android versions require it for any Bluetooth scan. The app does not read or store your location.
  • Notifications – to show battery alerts and the ongoing notification while background monitoring is on.
  • Internet – used only by the optional Remote monitoring feature described below.

Optional Remote monitoring

Remote monitoring is off by default. When you switch it on, on the phone that acts as the gateway:

  • Every 15 seconds the app uploads a summary of each battery: the battery name you chose, state of charge, voltage, current, power, cell voltages, maximum temperature, connection status and active alerts.
  • The data is stored in Google Firebase Realtime Database, operated by Google as our service provider. Each upload replaces the previous one; no history is kept on the server.
  • Firebase Authentication creates an anonymous account identifier for the app. It is not linked to your name, email or Google account. Google processes your IP address to deliver the service.
  • Anyone who has your 20-character site code can view the uploaded summary. Only share it with people you trust. Creating a new code locks out everyone who had the old one.
  • Switching Remote monitoring off, or creating a new code, deletes the uploaded data from the server.

Phones that view a remote site download that summary and create their own anonymous identifier in the same way.

Purchases

Purchases and subscriptions are processed by Google Play. We do not receive your payment details. The app asks Google Play on your device which products you own.

What we do not do

  • No account or sign-in.
  • No advertising, no advertising ID, no analytics and no crash-reporting services.
  • We do not sell or share your data.
  • The app only reads data from your BMS. It never changes BMS settings or passwords.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13.
